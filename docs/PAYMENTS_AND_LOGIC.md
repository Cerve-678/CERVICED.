## 1. How CERVICED gets paid

CERVICED runs on a client-side service fee. The provider is paid their service
price (or deposit); CERVICED adds its fee on top, charged to the client at
checkout. The provider never pays the fee and never sees it.

| Who | Pays / receives |
|---|---|
| Client | Service price (or deposit) + CERVICED service fee |
| Provider | The service price (or deposit) only |
| CERVICED | The service fee, never taken from the provider |

- Fee tiers on the full service value: under £100 = £1.99; £100–£300 = £3.99;
  over £300 = £5.99. A deposit-only checkout uses the same tiers on the full
  price.
- In a mixed basket the fee is spread across the pay-in-full bookings, pro-rata.
- Every amount is computed server-side from the checkout batch. The app never
  sends an amount, provider id or Stripe account id that the server trusts.
- **Late and no-show fees:** CERVICED takes no cut. Only Stripe's real card fee
  comes out of the provider's share.

## 2. The end-to-end money lifecycle

| # | Step | Owner | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Price & reserve | `prepare_checkout` | Validates the basket, holds each slot (`on_hold`, 10 min), writes the batch |
| 2 | Add fee | `apply_checkout_platform_fee` | Adds the service fee on top |
| 3 | Authorise | `create-payment-intent` | Re-reads the amount server-side; blocks providers who aren't payout-ready; if the booking has late/no-show fees, requires the client's consent and saves the card for later. Allows card (incl. Apple/Google Pay) plus **PayPal**, except when fees apply: then card only, since `booking-fee` charges saved cards only. Returns the allowed types to the app |
| 4 | Collect | Stripe `CardField` / Apple Pay / Google Pay / **PayPal**, inside the app's Complete Payment sheet | Client authorises payment. Card details go straight from Stripe's own field to Stripe; PayPal opens PayPal's page and returns to the app (`cerviced://stripe-redirect`); the PayPal button is hidden when the checkout has fees. A decline stays in the sheet and retries the same intent; closing the sheet cancels the unconfirmed intent |
| 5 | Capture & confirm | `finalize-payment-intent` | Captures, then promotes bookings `on_hold` → `pending`/`confirmed` |
| 6 | Hold payout | `create_held_payout_on_finalize` | Creates a held payout row. Release time = appointment end + **the provider's chosen hold** (`providers.payout_hold_days`: 1, 3, 7, 14 or 30 days; default 1) |
| 7 | Release | `process_due_payouts` → `release-payouts` | Every 15 min: re-checks the booking, skips it while a refund request is open*, takes out anything the provider owes*, transfers the rest to their Stripe account |
| 8 | Bank payout | Stripe | Stripe pays the provider's bank on its own default schedule (CERVICED doesn't set it) |

\* Live since 18 Oct (38c6e8b9).

**Changing payout timing:** a longer choice also pushes back payouts already
waiting. A shorter one applies to new bookings only, so a held payout can never
be pulled forward past an open refund or no-show window.

## 3. Accounting invariants

| Invariant | State |
|---|---|
| Provider payout ≤ client total − service fee (per booking) | Enforced (CHECK, since 18 Oct). Before that it said **=**, which every refund path's payout lowering violated; no money was affected |
| Exactly one payout per eligible booking, safe on retries | Enforced (unique key + idempotency) |
| Never pay out a cancelled / refunded / disputed booking | Enforced (re-checked at release) |
| Never pay out while the client's refund request is open | Enforced (re-checked at release) |
| Money taken back from a paid provider is never lost or taken twice | Enforced: a reversal Stripe refuses becomes a recorded shortfall; recovered from the next payout; given back if that payout fails |
| Every hold is at least 24h after the appointment | Enforced (CHECK + RPC whitelist + trigger floor) |

## 4. Provider payments experience

Payments & payouts has four sections: Overview, Booking payments, Payouts,
Payment settings.

- **Payment settings**: a "When you get
  paid" picker (24 hours / 3 / 7 / 14 / 30 days). The copy says the money then
  reaches their bank through Stripe a few working days later. The **Payouts**
  tab states the current timing and points to Payment settings to change it.
- **Never shown to providers:** CERVICED's fee, the client's fee-inclusive
  total, or CERVICED revenue. Providers see their own share, refunds and
  release status.
- Fee earnings (late/no-show) are listed on the Payments tab.

## 5. Refunds

| Situation | What happens |
|---|---|
| Provider declines / ignores a paid booking | Full auto-refund (per 3 Oct snapshot; not re-verified) |
| Client cancels inside the notice window | `apply-cancellation-refund` applies the provider's policy: the provider keeps the retained share, the client gets the rest. Both are notified (`cancellation_settled`) |
| Client cancels early | Full refund under the policy |
| Provider refunds (full or partial) | `refund-payment`; partial and repeat refunds allowed; CERVICED keeps its fee on a partial refund, returns it on a full one |
| Client asks for a refund | Request goes to the **provider** (`refund_requested`). The provider refunds or passes it to Cerviced, which resolves it. The client follows a timeline |
| Off-app payment | Not CERVICED's business: the client is told it's between them and the provider |

**Taking money back from a paid provider:** if the provider was already paid,
their Stripe transfer is reversed. If their Stripe balance can't cover it
(daily-payout providers are most exposed), the amount is recorded in
`provider_shortfalls` and taken out of their next booking payout. Gap: late/no-show fee payouts aren't used to settle shortfalls.

## 6. Late and no-show fees (live)

- Two fixed £ fees in the provider's policy: late arrival (after the grace
  period) and no-show. Each is capped at **50% of the service price**.
- Frozen onto the booking by a trigger at insert, from the live policy.
- The client agrees to them at checkout before their card is saved.
- The provider can charge **once, within 24 hours** of the appointment ending.
- The client disputes **to the provider**, who refunds or keeps it. CERVICED
  doesn't adjudicate.
- The fee's payout is held 24h, then paid minus Stripe's card fee. Paused while
  a dispute is open.
- A bank dispute (chargeback) reverses what the provider was paid, booking or
  fee.

**Legal flag:** the Terms §6 wording and checkout tick box are user-directed,
not lawyer-reviewed (LEGAL-COMPLIANCE-NOTES §17). A fee must read as a genuine
estimate of loss, not a penalty.

## 7. Security & the data boundary

- Provider fee visibility (UI): fixed. (Data level: per 3 Oct, a provider could
  still read the fee columns directly; not re-verified.)
- Shortfall functions are service-role only; providers can read their own
  shortfalls but not internal Stripe ids.
- Payout timing is written through `set_payout_hold_days()`; a direct owner
  update is bounded by the CHECK and never re-times held payouts.

## 8. Decisions locked in

| Decision | Call |
|---|---|
| Who chooses payout timing | The provider: 24h, 3, 7, 14 or 30 days (18 Oct). Stripe's bank schedule stays default |
| Late and no-show fees | In-app, 50% cap, 24h window, no CERVICED cut (13 Oct) |
| Fee disputes | Go to the provider, not CERVICED |
| Refund requests | Go to the provider first, then Cerviced if passed on |
| Payout while a refund request is open | Held until it closes (18 Oct) |
| Provider can't cover a reversal | Taken from their next payout (18 Oct) |
| CERVICED's fee on a partial refund | Kept; returned on a full refund |
| Off-app payments | Never tracked by the app |

## 9. Known gaps and launch blockers

- **Legal flag:** holding provider money up to 30 days isn't covered in the
  Terms or LEGAL-COMPLIANCE-NOTES, and Stripe Connect may limit how long a
  platform can delay transfers. Ask a solicitor and Stripe before the 14/30-day
  options go live.
- Late/no-show fee payouts aren't netted against shortfalls.
- A reschedule to a later date may not move the payout release date (per 3 Oct;
  not re-verified).
- Failed payouts aren't retried or alerted; there's no admin view for
  shortfalls.
