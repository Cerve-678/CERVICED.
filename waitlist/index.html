Why we're building it

Booking today isn't broken. It's just scattered.

Most people find a provider through a social media link, then piece the rest together by hand — working out what to actually book, retyping the same details for every new provider, and keeping track of everyone's policies themselves. It works. It's also the same manual effort, every single time. CERVICED puts all of it in one place.

What's different

Three things, specifically

The cart

Add services from several different providers — or several services from one — and check out once. Nothing else in this space works this way.

Built for how you find people

Discovery is visual and browsable, the way you already find providers online — not a directory bolted onto a calendar.

Your details, once

Your profile travels with you. No re-typing the same information for every new provider you try.

Two sides

Whichever side you're on

For providers

A real business presence — not a link in a bio.

  • A profile with your services, pricing and portfolio
  • Your own availability, buffers and blocked dates
  • Deposits, cancellation and reschedule policies you set
  • A booking dashboard, client history and promotions

For clients

Find someone, book them, get on with your day.

  • Browse and search independent providers near you
  • Save the ones you like and rebook in a tap
  • Manage, reschedule or cancel from one place
  • Reminders so nothing gets missed
