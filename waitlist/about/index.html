About Cerviced

Beauty has changed.
Booking hasn't.
Until now.

The best work in beauty is done by independent specialists: the nail tech with a home studio, the braider who travels to you, the aesthetician building a name one client at a time. Their clients don't book one appointment. They keep a routine, across several people, every few weeks. Cerviced is built for that routine.

Why we exist

Booking was built for the salon front desk

Most booking software started with one business, one diary and one checkout. That made sense when a salon did everything under one roof.

It doesn't fit beauty today. A client sees a different expert for hair, lashes, nails and skin, and each of those experts runs a business of one. The client has a routine. The provider has a business. Nothing connected the two.

Cerviced starts from both ends at once: the client's whole routine, and the independent provider's whole business. Every feature we build joins them up.

Maintenance reminders

After each appointment, Cerviced works out when you're due again and reminds you on the day. Book, mark it done or snooze a week. If you've already booked, it stays quiet.

Becca, your assistant

Ask in your own words. Becca finds providers, answers questions about your bookings and puts your last appointment back in your cart in one tap.

Waitlists that move

When a time you wanted frees up, it's held for you and you're told straight away. If you pass, it goes to the next person in line.

Rescheduling on a clock

Ask to move an appointment and the other side has 45 minutes to answer. You both see the deadline, so nobody waits days for a reply.

For providers

Your business, run like one

A profile on Cerviced is the whole business behind the chair: your rules, your brand, your diary and your money.

Your rules, applied for you

Set your own deposits, cancellation and rescheduling policies, and late and no-show fees. Clients agree to them before they pay, and the app applies them.

Your brand, not a template

Choose from curated colour collections and business-name fonts so your profile looks like your business.

Your diary, your release day

Let clients book a set time ahead, or open a month at a time on the day you choose.

Clients who arrive ready

Send info packs and forms before each appointment, so you know what you need to before anyone sits down.

Paid properly

Clients pay by card, Apple Pay, Google Pay or PayPal. Payouts go to your bank on the schedule you choose, and every tip goes to you in full.

Found by the right people

Clients find you by the service they want, where they are and the details that matter to them.

Where this goes

More specialists are going it alone, building loyal clients and real businesses on their own terms. They deserve tools as good as their work. Their clients deserve one place for everything they do to look and feel like themselves.

Cerviced is that place. We're opening soon, starting in the UK.
