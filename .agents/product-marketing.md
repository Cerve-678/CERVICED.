## The product (known)

CERVICED is a UK beauty and wellness app (prices in £) that connects clients
with **independent** beauty providers: lash techs, nail techs, brow artists,
and more. Most providers work from a home studio or their own small space, not
a chain salon. Clients and providers use the same app, each with their own side.

What a client can do today:
- **Explore**: a Pinterest-style feed of real work from real providers.
- **Collections and moodboards**: save the looks they want.
- **Provider pages**: services, prices, portfolio, reviews, and each provider's own page style.
- **Cart and checkout**: book several services, including across several providers, and pay in the app.
- **Beauty Profile**: their lash, nail, skin, and brow details, which the providers they book can see.
- **Maintenance reminders**: a nudge when infills or a refresh are due.
- **Your Providers**: the artists they keep going back to.
- **Becca**: an in-app assistant for finding and booking.
- **Waitlists**: a place in the queue when a provider is full.

What a provider gets: their own branded page and web link (`cerviced.co/@handle`),
bookings, deposits, reminders, waitlist, client history and notes, analytics,
and payouts.

## Positioning (decided 2026-11-27)

**CERVICED is more than a booking platform. It's where the look begins, and where it's remembered.**

The story runs in two halves, and booking is just the moment between them:

1. **Where looks begin** (discovery). You see a look, save it, collect it, then
   find and book the person who did it. *"saw it. saved it. booked it."*
2. **Your beauty, remembered** (relationship). CERVICED keeps your lash map,
   patch test, artist, and when you're next due, so the look stays yours.
   *"it remembers you."*

The joined-up line: *"saw it. saved it. kept it."*

What we are **not**: a calendar, a salon software tool, a discount marketplace,
or a directory. Don't market the booking screen. Market the life around it.

## Who we're for

### Clients (primary audience for social)
- **To verify:** UK women aged roughly 18 to 34 who book lashes, nails, or
  brows every 2 to 6 weeks, find looks on Pinterest and TikTok, and currently
  book through Instagram DMs.
- What they want **(to verify):** to find *that exact look* and the person who
  does it well nearby, to stop re-explaining their preferences every visit, and
  not to lose track of when they're due.

### Providers (second audience)
- **Known:** independent beauty pros who run their own business.
- What they want **(to verify):** a page that looks like their brand, fewer DMs,
  fewer no-shows, deposits handled for them, and clients who come back.

## Competitors and alternatives (to verify)

- **Instagram DMs and link-in-bio booking**: the real incumbent for independents.
- **Fresha, Booksy, Treatwell**: booking-first platforms. They market the
  calendar and the discount; we deliberately don't.
- **Pinterest and TikTok themselves**: where the look is found today, with no
  bridge to the person who does it.

## Brand voice (known)

- Lowercase on social (`cerviced`, never `CERVICED`, in captions and graphics).
  Product screens, legal pages, and the logo keep capitals.
- Short, plain, warm. Friendly lines a person would actually say, never
  internal or product jargon ("hat", "flow", "capability", "user").
- UK English and £: colour, favourite, appointment, "infills".
- Confident and quiet, never shouting. No exclamation-mark pile-ups, no
  "revolutionising beauty", no AI tells ("it's not X, it's Y", broetry).
- Speak to one person: "your artist", "your lash map", "you're due".

## Claims we can and can't make

- Only claim features that exist today (list above). Anything on a branch or
  roadmap is not marketable yet.
- **Health-adjacent data** (patch tests, pregnancy safety, contraindications,
  aftercare) is sensitive. Never imply CERVICED gives medical advice, makes a
  treatment safe, or verifies anyone's health. Any copy touching it goes past
  the `cerviced-legal-flagger` agent first.
- Paid or gifted creator content must be disclosed (#ad) under UK ASA/CAP
  rules. Flag, don't decide. That's `cerviced-legal-flagger` territory too.
- Never use another brand's or creator's images, footage, or sounds in our own
  posts without rights. Pinterest and TikTok finds are reference only.
