## The world: A + C

Two halves of one story (see positioning in `product-marketing.md`):

| | **C · where looks begin** | **A · your beauty, remembered** |
|---|---|---|
| Feeling | Collage-y, playful, Pinterest energy, the thrill of finding it | Intimate, soft, personal, a beauty diary |
| Shows | Saved faces, polaroids, looks on a phone screen, the mirror selfie on the way out | Fresh results up close, linen and morning coffee, the warm mug in the chair, "you're due" nails, the same artist every visit |
| Lines | *saw it. saved it. booked it.* · *the look has a name.* | *it remembers you.* · *same time next month?* |

Board sections, in story order: **the look → the result → the ritual →
remembered**, plus **palette & type**.

## What we borrow from rhode (and what we don't)

Take:
- The result as the hero, shot like an object: one subject, plain soft ground, no clutter.
- Extreme close-ups worn on the body (lash, nail, brow), never just held up.
- Texture as the advert: gloss, wax, gel, linen. rhode uses food; we use the tools and materials of the craft.
- The lowercase name as a big graphic, cropped across the frame. Very few other words.
- Colour pulled from the subject, not laid on top.

Don't take: celebrity-led faces, product packshots (we sell people and results,
not tubes), or anything that reads as a product catalogue.

## Palette

Seven named shades. Use one or two per piece, never all of them.

| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| linen | `#FBF2EC` | Default light ground |
| blush | `#E8CBCF` | Soft fields, A-side warmth |
| rose | `#AF9197` | Accent (also the app's accent) |
| plum | `#3F1E36` | Type on light, deep fields |
| cocoa | `#5C4033` | Warm dark, texture shots |
| mist | `#E5ECF4` | C-side cool, screens and discovery |
| night | `#17151A` | Dark mode ground |

Every graphic needs both a light and a dark version.

## Type

- **Social and pins:** DM Sans 500, lowercase, tight tracking (about −4%). Big
  and confident; one idea per frame.
- **Moments:** Prata (serif) for an occasional line such as *"same time next month?"*.
  At most one serif line per piece.
- **Not for social:** Bakbak is the app's numerals face and the website's
  display face. Keep it out of feed graphics.

## Imagery rules

- Real people, real results, real providers. Diverse skin tones and ages by default.
- Close, still, natural light. Dewy, not airbrushed. No smiling-at-camera stock.
- Phone-shot is fine, even preferred, for C (discovery feels native). A is softer and slower.
- App screens appear rarely and small, as a prop in a hand or on linen, never as the hero.
- **Rights:** pins and TikToks we find are mood reference only. Our own posts
  use content we shot, or creator content we have written permission to use.
